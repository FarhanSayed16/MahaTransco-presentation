# Live Writing Demo

## Objective
Prove iteration: one advisory → four audiences in minutes. Prompt engineering is follow-ups, not shouting.

## Talking Points
- Paste English circular; let the room watch generation in silence.
- Same chat: Marathi → Hindi → WhatsApp (<40 words) → social (<280).
- “Judgment on what to say stays with you — retyping dies.”
- Ask: would you sign a cleaned English version?
- Remind: no invented feeders / phone numbers — `[VERIFY]` is a feature.

## Actions / Hotkeys
- Use on-screen step buttons; Copy + Open ChatGPT/Claude.
- Keep one conversation open so context carries.
- `D` demo tray if needed; `Space` when done.

---
*Suggested time allocation: ~8 minutes*
