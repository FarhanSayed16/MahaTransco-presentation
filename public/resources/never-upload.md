# Never Upload Checklist (DPDP practical)

Treat free public AI tools as an **open phone line**.

## Do not paste / upload
- [ ] Confidential, restricted, or FOUO government files  
- [ ] Aadhaar numbers or other government ID data  
- [ ] Police investigation records, FIR content, or evidence  
- [ ] Cabinet documents or pre-decisional policy drafts  
- [ ] Internal financial reports / budgets not yet public  
- [ ] Medical records of any individual  
- [ ] Citizen personal data combinations (name + address + phone + ID)  
- [ ] Vendor-confidential material  
- [ ] Tender evaluation sheets or bid documents before award  

## Habit question
> Would I be comfortable if this exact text appeared on a public website tomorrow?

If **no**, it does not go into a free AI tool. Use the correct tier (enterprise / on-prem / air-gapped).

## Legal anchors (presenter recall)
- DPDP Act, 2023 — data fiduciary duties on citizen personal data  
- Official Secrets / departmental confidentiality — AI creates no exception  
- IndiaAI Governance Guidelines (Nov 2025) — Do No Harm; departmental judgment
