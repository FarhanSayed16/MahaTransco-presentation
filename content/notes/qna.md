# Q&A Preparation — MahaTransco AI Workshop

Use during Scene 28 / open floor. Under-promise; be precise.

---

**Q: Is AI-generated content usable in an official document as-is?**  
**A:** No. Treat every AI output as a first draft. Same review, fact-check, and sign-off as any human draft. Value = time to first draft, not skipping accountability.

**Q: What happens to data we type into these tools?**  
**A:** On free/consumer tiers, inputs may be used to improve models unless you are on an enterprise plan that guarantees otherwise. That is why confidential and citizen personal data never go into free tools.

**Q: Is there a government-approved tool we should use instead?**  
**A:** Evolving under IndiaAI Mission / departmental IT–NIC guidance. Safe default beyond non-sensitive drafting: enterprise with no-training guarantee, or on-prem for sensitive data. Check your IT cell for current approvals.

**Q: Can this replace translation / design / IT cells?**  
**A:** No. It removes first-draft delay. Humans still finalise anything official, legal, technical, or citizen-facing at scale.

**Q: How do we know it isn't making things up?**  
**A:** You don't by default — that is hallucination. Never treat AI-stated facts, numbers, dates, or citations as verified until checked. NotebookLM helps because it cites pages.

**Q: What about officers with weaker English or tech skills?**  
**A:** Translation and plain-language reformatting are strengths. The skill that matters is asking clearly — not typing speed or perfect English.

**Q: Isn't this hype that will be outdated in six months?**  
**A:** Specific apps will change. The durable skill is: which type of tool, how to prompt, how to verify.

**Q: What's the actual cost?**  
**A:** Demos use free tiers. Cost appears when you deliberately buy enterprise privacy guarantees or on-prem deployment — procurement decisions, not casual spend.

**Q: Who is accountable if an AI-assisted document has an error?**  
**A:** The same officer who would be accountable without AI. AI is a drafting aid, not a signatory.

**Q: Can AI analyse our grievance / scheme data?**  
**A:** Yes, high value — but outputs are hypotheses to verify, not automatic conclusions for policy or enforcement. Never upload restricted datasets to public tools.

**Q: Someone already pasted sensitive data into a public tool — now what?**  
**A:** Flag to IT / data-protection contact immediately. Early reporting matters more than blame.

**Q: Are you selling something?**  
**A:** No. Free tiers, not a procurement pitch, no commercial stake in the tools shown. Goal: do not miss time-saving patterns on real paperwork.
