# The Promise + Poll

## Objective
Audience self-diagnoses the personal-vs-official AI gap, then hears the three takeaways.

## Talking Points
- Reveal poll questions one at a time; wait for hands.
- Typical pattern: many on #1, fewer #2, few on #3.
- “That gap is why this session exists.”
- Then land the three skills: draft · summarise with citations · outreach media safely.

## Actions / Hotkeys
- Use on-screen “Next question”.
- `Space` to advance when finished.

---
*Suggested time allocation: ~4 minutes*
