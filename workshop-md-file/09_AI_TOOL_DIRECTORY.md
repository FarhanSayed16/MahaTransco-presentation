## **09 — AI Tool Directory** 

Every tool referenced in this kit, what it's for, and notes on free-tier access. Check current free-tier limits close to the session date — these change often. 

### **Text Generation** 

**ChatGPT** (chat.openai.com) — general drafting, translation, reformatting. Free tier available; used in the main text demo. 

- **Gemini** (gemini.google.com) — also strong for text; overlaps with ChatGPT. 

- **Claude** (claude.ai) — strong for longer, more careful drafting and document analysis; useful alternative to mention. 

- **Perplexity** (perplexity.ai) — best when you need an answer grounded in current web sources with citations, rather than pure generation. 

### **Image Generation** 

- **Gemini** (native image generation) — free tier, good general quality. 

- **Nano Banana** — Google's fast image-generation model, accessible via Gemini; strong for quick photorealistic concepts. 

- **ChatGPT Images** — image generation inside ChatGPT. 

### **Video Generation** 

**Google Veo** (via Gemini/Flow) — check current free-credit availability before the session. 

- **Pika** (pika.art) — free-tier short video clips. 

- **Runway** (runwayml.com) — free-tier available, strong for short cinematic clips. _(Video tool free tiers change fastest of any category — re-verify 1–2 days before the session and have a backup clip regardless.)_ 

### **Document & Presentation Intelligence** 

- **NotebookLM** (notebooklm.google.com) — Google's document-grounded AI notebook; free; core tool for the document demo. Cites sources/pages. 

**Gamma** (gamma.app) — turns outlines/documents into designed slide decks quickly; free tier available. 

- **Canva AI** (canva.com) — AI-assisted design and presentation tools, useful for polishing the final PPT deck itself. 

### **Research** 

**Perplexity** — real-time, cited web research. 

- **NotebookLM** — research grounded in your own uploaded documents specifically. 

### **Audio/Speech** 

- **ElevenLabs** (elevenlabs.io) — text-to-speech and voice synthesis; mention in the deepfake section as the category of tool that makes voice cloning possible — do not demo voice cloning live. 

### **Coding (mention briefly if asked, not part of the main demo flow)** 

**ChatGPT** , **Claude** , **Gemini** — all capable of code generation/explanation; relevant if the audience asks about automating internal reporting tools. 

### **Meetings/Notes** 

**NotebookLM** — can also summarize uploaded meeting transcripts. 

### **Quick-reference: which tool for which job** 

|**Need**|**Tool**|
|---|---|
|Draft/translate/reformat text|ChatGPT|
