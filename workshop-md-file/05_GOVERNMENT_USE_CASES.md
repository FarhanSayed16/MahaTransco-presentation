## **05 — Government Use Cases for IAS Officers** 

55 practical, department-relevant AI applications. Use this as a handout appendix or as ad-lib material if the audience asks "what else can this do for my department specifically." 

Each entry: **Use case — which AI capability — one-line note.** 

### **A. Administrative Drafting & Correspondence** 

1. Drafting government orders and notifications from bullet-point instructions — Text generation — always have legal/language cell review before issue. 

2. Drafting and reformatting official circulars for multiple channels (notice, WhatsApp, SMS, social media) — Text generation — as demoed live. 

3. Translating circulars and notices into regional languages — Text generation — verify with a native-language officer for legal/technical terms. 

4. Drafting replies to routine correspondence and D.O. letters — Text generation — draft only, officer finalizes tone and content. 

5. Proofreading and formal-tone correction of officer-written drafts — Text generation — useful for non-native English writers. 

6. Preparing minutes of meetings from raw notes or a recording transcript — Text + speech-to-text — verify names/decisions against your own notes. 

7. Drafting press releases and public statements — Text generation — always route through PRO/media cell before release. 

8. Generating standard reply templates for recurring citizen queries — Text generation — good for grievance cells and call centers. 

### **B. Document & Report Intelligence** 

9. Summarizing long policy documents, committee reports, or audit reports — NotebookLM/RAG — cites page numbers for verification. 

10. Extracting all deadlines and time-bound actions from a scheme guideline document — NotebookLM — useful before a review meeting. 

11. Generating an FAQ document from a new scheme's guidelines for field staff — NotebookLM — reduces repeated queries to the nodal office. 

12. Preparing a one-page briefing note for a senior officer from a long file — NotebookLM — saves reading time before meetings. 

13. Comparing two versions of a policy document to identify what changed — Document intelligence — useful during draft revisions. 

14. Converting a lengthy report into a slide deck outline — Gamma/NotebookLM — quick first draft for review committees. 

15. Digitizing and summarizing scanned/handwritten old files (with OCR + text models) — Vision + text — pilot only, always human-verify historical/legal content. 

### **C. Data, Grievances & Analytics** 

16. Sentiment/latent-intent analysis on citizen grievance text (e.g., CPGRAMS data) to spot recurring pain points — NLP analysis — mirrors the sentiment work in your own market-research plan. 

17. Clustering grievances by theme/department automatically before manual triage — NLP classification — reduces manual sorting time. 

18. Identifying districts/blocks with unusually rising complaint volumes — Reasoning/analysis — flag for review, not automatic action. 

19. Drafting a root-cause hypothesis list from grievance patterns for a review meeting — Reasoning model — hypotheses only, needs field verification. 

20. Summarizing public consultation feedback (hundreds of submissions) into key themes — NLP/document intelligence — useful for policy consultations. 

21. Preparing survey questionnaires in plain-language, respondent-friendly format — Text generation — similar to your own "Ask/Why" questionnaire revision. 

22. First-pass coding of open-ended survey responses into categories — NLP classification — always spot-check a sample manually. 

### **D. Disaster Management & Emergency Communication** 

23. Drafting weather/disaster advisory circulars in multiple languages and formats — Text generation — as demoed live. 

24. Generating awareness posters/visuals for evacuation routes or relief camps — Image generation — first-draft only, verify factual details (locations, numbers) manually. 

25. Producing short awareness/training video clips for field teams — Video generation — good for internal briefing, not public broadcast without review. 

26. Drafting SOPs for emergency response from past incident reports — Document intelligence — cross-check against actual approved SOPs. 

27. Summarizing incoming field reports during an active disaster into a consolidated situation report — NLP summarization — speed matters here; 

always verify critical numbers (casualties, relief stock) manually. 

28. Real-time translation of distress calls/messages from citizens in regional languages or dialects — Speech/text translation — verify before dispatching response resources. 

### **E. Public Health & Welfare Schemes** 

29. Drafting vaccination/health-camp awareness content in local languages — Text + image generation. 

30. Summarizing health scheme uptake data to identify low-coverage blocks — Data analysis. 

31. Generating FAQ material for a new welfare scheme for Anganwadi/ASHA workers — Text generation — plain-language output is a genuine strength here. 

32. Drafting nutrition/health advisory content tailored to local dietary context — Text generation — always route through medical/technical review. 

### **F. Law & Order, Investigation Support (non-sensitive uses only)** 

33. Drafting standard administrative orders related to law and order (prohibitory orders, permissions) — Text generation — legal review mandatory. 

34. Summarizing publicly available case law or legal provisions for quick reference — Document intelligence — never a substitute for legal opinion. 

35. Verifying whether a circulating image/video/audio is likely AI-generated before acting on it — Deepfake detection habits — see 07_DEEPFAKE_AND_AI_MISUSE.md . _(Note: never upload actual investigation records, FIR content, or evidence into any public AI tool — see 06_DATA_PRIVACY.md .)_ 

### **G. Revenue, Land Records & RTI** 

36. Drafting responses to routine RTI applications from standard templates — Text generation — legal officer must approve final response. 

37. Summarizing land record disputes from case history for a hearing briefing — Document intelligence — verify against the physical record. 

38. Translating land record notices for affected citizens in regional languages — Text generation. 

39. Drafting public notice content for land acquisition/consultation processes — Text generation. 

### **H. Education & Skill Development** 

40. Drafting simplified explainer content for new education policy circulars aimed at teachers/parents — Text generation. 

41. Generating quiz/training material for staff training programs — Text generation. 

42. Summarizing school inspection reports across a district into common themes — Document intelligence. 

### **I. Urban Governance & Infrastructure** 

43. Drafting citizen communication for infrastructure project disruptions (road closures, water supply interruption) — Text generation. 

44. Generating before/after concept visuals for proposed public infrastructure (parks, markets) for consultation purposes — Image generation — clearly label as a concept visual, not an architectural plan. 

45. Summarizing contractor/vendor progress reports into a consolidated dashboard-ready format — Document intelligence. 

### **J. Internal Office Productivity** 

46. Preparing agenda and briefing packs for review meetings from multiple source documents — Document intelligence. 

47. Drafting internal office memos and standard operating notices — Text generation. 

48. Summarizing long email threads before a meeting — Text summarization. 

49. Preparing a first-draft dashboard narrative from raw data exports (e.g., disbursement/scheme progress numbers) — Text + data analysis — mirrors BI dashboard narrative-writing work. 

50. Generating training material/slide decks for onboarding new staff — Gamma/text generation. 

### **K. Public Communication & Trust** 

51. Drafting citizen-facing explainer content on new government digital services — Text generation. 

52. Producing multilingual audio versions of important public notices — Speech synthesis — always disclose as AI-generated per IT Rules 2026 where applicable. 

53. Fact-checking a viral claim against official sources before issuing a public rebuttal — Verification workflow, not a single "AI check." 

54. Drafting a public statement addressing misinformation/deepfake content circulating about the department — Text generation + 07_DEEPFAKE_AND_AI_MISUSE.md protocol. 

55. Preparing a plain-language "myth vs fact" handout for a scheme facing public confusion — Text generation. 

### **Standing caveat for every use case above** 

AI drafts, summarizes, translates, and visualizes faster than a human first pass. It does not verify facts, does not carry legal accountability, and should never touch confidential, personal, or investigative data on a public tool. Every use case above assumes a human officer reviews and owns the final output.
