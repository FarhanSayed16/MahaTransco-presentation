# **11 — Appendix: Glossary & Cheat Sheet** 

## **Glossary (plain-language, one line each)** 

- **AI Agent:** An AI system that takes multi-step actions toward a goal, not just answering one question. 

- **Context Window:** How much text (input + output) a model can hold in memory during one conversation. 

- **Deepfake:** Realistic AI-generated or AI-altered audio/video/image of a real person, often used to deceive. 

- **DPDP Act, 2023:** India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, governing personal data handling. 

- **Enterprise AI:** Paid business-tier AI subscription, typically with a no-training-on-your-data guarantee. 

- **Few-shot Prompting:** Giving the AI examples of the desired output before asking for a new one. 

- **Function Calling:** A model triggering an external action (lookup, API call) rather than just producing text. 

- **Hallucination:** Confident-sounding but false or fabricated AI output. 

- **IndiaAI Mission:** India's national umbrella program (under MeitY) for AI adoption, safety, and infrastructure. 

- **India AI Governance Guidelines:** National framework (unveiled Nov 2025) built on a "Do No Harm" principle and human-centricity. 

- **IT Rules Amendment 2026:** February 2026 amendment bringing deepfakes/synthetic media under mandatory labelling and faster takedown rules. 

- **MCP (Model Context Protocol):** An open standard letting AI models connect to external tools/data sources consistently. 

- **On-premise / Local LLM:** An AI model run entirely on an organization's own servers, with no data leaving the network. 

- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** AI that retrieves real documents/sources before generating an answer, improving accuracy and traceability. 

- **Reasoning Model:** An AI model that works through multi-step logic before answering, useful for analysis tasks. 

- **Role Prompting:** Telling the AI what role/persona to adopt (e.g., "You are a press officer") to shape its tone and output. 

- **SGI (Synthetically Generated Information):** The Indian IT Rules' technology-neutral legal term for deepfakes/AI-altered media. 

- **Temperature:** A setting controlling how creative vs. predictable AI output is. 

- **Token:** The small text unit (roughly ¾ of a word) that AI models process and are billed by. 

- **Zero-shot Prompting:** Asking the AI directly, with no examples given.

**Before you trust an AI answer:** verify facts, dates, numbers, and citations against a real source. 

**Before you forward anything questionable:** pause on urgency · check the source · reverse-image-search · verify independently · escalate properly. 

**The closing line:** AI will not replace officers. Officers who understand AI will outperform officers who don't.
