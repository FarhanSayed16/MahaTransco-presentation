## 03 — Live Demo Guide

Every prompt is pre-written below. Do not type live — copy-paste only. Run every demo once tonight/tomorrow morning and save a screenshot of the output in a backups/ folder on your laptop and phone, in case venue Wi-Fi fails.

## 0. Opening Hook Image (run this first, before the session — it's Slide 1, not a live demo)

Tool: Gemini, Nano Banana, or ChatGPT Images

Prompt: "Create a highly realistic, documentary-style photograph of a busy Indian district collectorate office during monsoon season. Officers at desks reviewing files, natural window light, muted government office colors, no visible faces looking at camera, photojournalistic style, no text or watermark in the image."

Backup: Save 2–3 output variations. Pick the most convincing one the night before.

## 1. Text Generation — Tool: ChatGPT

Main prompt: "Draft an official government circular informing citizens that heavy rainfall is expected over the next three days. Keep the language formal and concise, suitable for a District Administration notice."

Follow-up 1 — regional language: "Convert this into Marathi, keeping it formal enough for an official notice."

Follow-up 2 — regional language: "Now translate the original into Hindi, same tone."

Follow-up 3 — channel reformat: "Rewrite this as a short WhatsApp advisory message, under 40 words, suitable for a citizen broadcast group."

Follow-up 4 — channel reformat: "Rewrite this as a tweet for the district administration's official account, under 280 characters, with an appropriate hashtag."

Talking point while it generates: Don't fill silence with filler — let the room watch the text stream in. It's more convincing live than described. Backup: Screenshot each of the five outputs (original + 4 follow-ups) in order.

## 2. Image Generation — Tool: Gemini / Nano Banana

Main prompt: "Create a realistic disaster relief camp in an Indian village after flooding. Government officers coordinating rescue operations, helicopters delivering supplies, natural lighting, documentary style."

Optional second prompt (if time allows): "Create an awareness poster encouraging citizens to get vaccinated at a government health camp, warm colors, Hindi text space left blank for local customization, clean minimal design."

Backup: Save both outputs as high-resolution images, not screenshots, so they display sharp on the projector if you switch to backup.

## 3. Video Generation — Tool: Google Veo, Pika, or Runway (whichever has free credits available closer to the date — check 09_AI_TOOL_DIRECTORY.md )

Main prompt: "Generate a 10-second cinematic video showing district administration responding to floods — rescue teams, emergency vehicles, and drone surveillance, natural daylight, realistic documentary tone."

Timing note: Video generation is the slowest demo (can take 1–5 minutes depending on the tool and server load). Two options: - Option A (recommended): Generate it the night before, play the finished clip live, and simply explain "this took under two minutes to generate." - Option B: Start the generation live at the very beginning of Phase 4 (while running the text demo) so it's ready by the time you reach the video segment.

Backup: A fully rendered backup clip, downloaded locally, not dependent on internet — play this if anything looks uncertain.

## 4. Document & Presentation Intelligence — Tool: NotebookLM

Setup: Upload a real (non-confidential) government report or a public policy PDF — annual report, scheme guideline document, or a public consultation paper works well. Do NOT upload anything confidential (see 06_DATA_PRIVACY.md ).

Prompt 1 — summarize: "Summarize this document in five bullet points for a senior officer who has not read it."


Prompt 2 — extract deadlines: "List every deadline, date, or time-bound action mentioned in this document, with the page number for each."

Prompt 3 — FAQ: "Generate a list of 8 likely questions a field officer would ask about this document, with short answers."

Prompt 4 — briefing note: "Draft a one-page briefing note for a senior officer based on this document, in formal government style."

Talking point: Point out that NotebookLM cites the page number for every claim — this is the single most important feature to highlight, because it directly answers the "can I trust this" concern this audience will have.

Optional extension — Gamma: If time allows, show the same document converted into a slide outline using Gamma ("Convert this summary into a 6-slide presentation outline"). This closes the loop on "doc → PPT" that was part of the original ask.

Backup: Screenshot the summary, deadline list, FAQ, and briefing note outputs from your test run.

## Troubleshooting checklist (keep this on a second screen/phone during the session)

- Internet drops: Switch immediately to the corresponding backup screenshot/video — do not apologize repeatedly, just say "Let me show you the output from my test run this morning" and move on.

- Tool is slow to respond: Have the backup ready before you even start typing — never let the room watch a loading spinner for more than 10– 15 seconds.

- A generated output looks poor quality: Don't re-roll live in front of the room more than once — switch to the backup and note "results can vary run to run, here's a clean version."

- An officer asks to type their own prompt live: Welcome this — it's the best possible proof the tool works. Have a spare 2 minutes buffered in your timing for exactly this (built into Phase 4's 40-minute block).

- A demo touches a sensitive real name/place: Keep all demo content fictional or clearly generic (a "District X" style example), never a real ongoing case or a named real official.
